# Using the Swiss pilot

This guide describes the current citizen-facing experience. For exact corpus coverage and review status, see [Current status](STATUS.md). Technical contributors should start with [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and the [documentation index](README.md).

- **Topics & votes:** open a public issue, read the ballot context and compare attributed arguments. The five pilot votes are historical examples.
- **Reading / Research:** stay on the same issue; Research keeps source inspection close to your reading. All topics & votes returns to the collection.
- **Parliament:** select a proposal or person. Read the original interventions and open their official sources. The status belongs to a parliamentary proposal, not necessarily a public ballot.
- **Read the debate:** search original French, German or Italian wording. Results include text even if no playable extract exists. When available, Watch this extract opens a machine-timed recording; timing still needs human review.
- **Cleisthenes:** ask a question in your preferred language. Check the source beneath each answer. The scope label tells you whether you are asking about one record or all imported sources. An overview covers a small selection of passages, not the entire debate.
- **People:** inspect canton, chamber, party, declared background and available votes. Load official profile & vote history retrieves additional public records. Missing votes are not abstentions, and an absent portrait is not replaced with a fabricated face.
- **Save and export:** ordinary login is needed for saved evidence. In Research, select evidence for a cited briefing. Contact drafts can be edited and opened in your own email application; the pilot does not send them.

## What is available?
The pilot has a substantial official-text snapshot but a small processed-video collection. Text search works without the GPU. AI answers, machine translation and experimental image search need their corresponding service connections. Visual similarity is an experimental tool, not proof that an event occurred or a statement was made. It belongs under Advanced video tools.

The app is an independent prototype, not a government service, a complete parliamentary archive or a way to cast votes. Follow official links for complete documents. If an AI answer is unavailable, continue reading its sources or try a more specific question.
