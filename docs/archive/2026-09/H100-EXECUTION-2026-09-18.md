## Party seed pipeline executed

Imported six official party pages, one per party, through `config/party-sources.json`. Raw HTML snapshots are hash-addressed; extracted text retains URL, publisher, language, retrieval time, unknown publication date and explicit review status. Import is resumable; `--refresh` retrieves a new version. Explicit content selectors avoid silently treating navigation as body text. The PVL source now uses the observed canonical `pvl.swiss` destination.

On GPU 1, the pinned embedding model processed all six documents into ten chunks. Results were copied back and validated against the source hashes with `node scripts/validate-party-embeddings.mjs`. No new long-running model service was installed. Original party text is labelled party self-description, never an independent assessment or a member's personal position.

A small H100 retrieval smoke test used 15 English/French/German topic queries over the seed corpus: the intended document ranked first for 12/15 and within the first three for 15/15. This is a deliberately small known-topic check, not a balanced benchmark; it has no lexical baseline yet, excludes Italian and does not demonstrate broad political-question quality. Results: `artifacts/party-retrieval-smoke.json`.

The corpus is **staging only**. The six pages cover different types of material and are not comparable complete programmes. They have NOT been silently added to live answers or published profiles.
